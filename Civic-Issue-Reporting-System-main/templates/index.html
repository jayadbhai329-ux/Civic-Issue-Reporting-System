HOW IT WORKS

From Complaint to Resolution

A transparent 4-step workflow connecting citizens directly with administration.

01

Create Account

Register as a citizen with your email and phone number to track your submissions anytime.

02

Report Issue

Select category, describe the problem, pinpoint location, and attach clear photo evidence.

03

Admin Review

Municipal administrators review tickets, assign field teams, and update progress in real time.

04

Track Resolution

Monitor your issue through Pending, In Progress, to Resolved status with official notes.

SUPPORTED CATEGORIES

Common Civic Issues We Help Address

Our platform supports multiple municipal problem types for rapid routing.

🛣️

Roads & Potholes

Damaged tarmac, potholes, unpaved surfaces, or open trenches on roads.

💡

Streetlights

Faulty poles, flickering fixtures, or dark public walkways and avenues.

🗑️

Garbage Collection

Overflowing bins, uncollected trash heaps, and open waste dumping.

💧

Water Supply & Leakage

Broken pipelines, contaminated tap water, or low municipal water pressure.

🚰

Drainage & Sewage

Clogged storm drains, sewage overflows, and rainwater waterlogging.

🌳

Public Parks & Spaces

Broken park benches, fallen trees, damaged playground equipment.
